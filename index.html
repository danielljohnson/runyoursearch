Run Your Search

Your job search needs a routine.

Most AI job search tools help you do more. Find more matches. Apply to more jobs. Rewrite more resumes. Send more messages. But is that actually working?

Run Your Search is a new job search tool for software engineers, built to help you figure out what to do next, learn how to do it well, and make it count.

You put in the reps. Run Your Search programs the training.

Launching October 2026. Waitlist members get in first.

Why I’m building this.

I’ve spent 21 years in software: a Netflix engineer, a director of engineering, a programming instructor, and a career coach. I interviewed engineers at Netflix, trained other interviewers, and designed interview loops. Through all of it, the best engineers I worked with didn’t just have talent. They had a routine.

I learned that before tech. I attended the country’s top ski academy, trained alongside Olympic athletes, and later coached racing myself. Sports taught me what consistent training does for performance. A job search works the same way.

I’ve coached 90+ engineers through their searches, and ran my own in 2026. I couldn’t find a tool that did what I wanted, so I’m building the one I’d want if I started a new search tomorrow. It’s opinionated. It won’t do your job search for you. It gives you a training plan, shows you how to put in the reps, and changes the plan based on what’s actually happening in your search.

Run Your Search · Routine beats luck.
