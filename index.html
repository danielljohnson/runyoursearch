How it works

Six steps, then it loops.

Assess → Kickoff → Routine → Reps → Review → Repeat

  1. 01

    Assess

    Complete an assessment covering your goal, timeline, current search, and the time you actually have.

  2. 02

    Kickoff

    A 30-minute call with me to define the initial approach and what we are training for.

  3. 03

    Get your routine

    I personally write your first week’s routine and the metrics you’ll track.

  4. 04

    Put in the reps

    Run the routine during the week, with direct async access to me whenever you’re stuck.

  5. 05

    Review

    Fill out a short end-of-week summary. What got done, what worked, what came up.

  6. 06

    Repeat

    I read what happened and program next week around it. Every week, a new routine.

An interview appears next Thursday? The routine changes.

Applications aren’t producing conversations? The routine changes.

Networking starts producing leads? The routine changes.
